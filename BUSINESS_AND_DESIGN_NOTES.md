# Business requirements translated into the design

## Business objective

Green Road is positioned as a private documentary studio, not a general video-production company. The website's job is to build enough emotional trust and operational confidence for affluent families, founders, and family businesses to begin a confidential discovery conversation.

The primary journey implemented is:

**Story → Trust → Process → Film → Conversation**

## Strategic design choices

- Story Territories describe **what** the client wants preserved.
- Production Levels describe **how much narrative and production depth** the story requires.
- Public prices, commodity packages, gear lists, discounts, and event-video language are intentionally absent.
- Privacy is treated as a primary buying criterion.
- The visual system uses carbon blue, mineral silver, and restrained champagne gold.
- Editorial typography, negative space, film-frame compositions, grain-like texture, and controlled motion create a premium documentary atmosphere.
- Contact fields ask only enough to begin an informed, personal conversation.

## Conversion architecture

The primary call to action is **Tell us your story**. The site repeatedly supports it with:

- emotional recognition through Story Territories;
- scope clarity through three Film Levels;
- risk reduction through process and privacy;
- objection handling through the FAQ;
- direct access to contact details and a concise inquiry form.

## Information still required from the client

- Confirmed email, phone number, and full studio/mailing address
- Google Business Profile URL or Place ID
- Approved film stills, archival materials, trailers, and portraits
- Filmmaker biography, awards, festivals, and selected credits
- Permission-cleared testimonials and portfolio stories
- Form delivery destination (email, HubSpot, HoneyBook, etc.)
- Privacy policy, terms, and legal copy
